Transmission notes
  • Each frame contains a preamble, a 16-bit payload length, UTF-8 text, and a CRC-16/CCITT checksum.
  • The receiver compares FFT energy near 1200 Hz and 2200 Hz to classify each bit.
  • Room echo, speaker and microphone response, automatic gain control, and timing drift can cause errors. Treat this as an experiment, not a reliable communication system.
